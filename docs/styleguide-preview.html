The rules that decide arguments

The Rationed Green RuleSettled Forest appears in exactly three places on a screen: the header, the primary action, and the current selection. A fourth means one of them is not actually primary.
The Two Greens Are Not Interchangeable RuleSettled Forest means Splyt. Signal Green means live right now. Swapping them makes chrome look like status.
The Money Is The Headline RuleThe currency value is the largest and heaviest element in its section. If a label outweighs the number, the hierarchy is inverted.
The Two Steps RuleA value sits one size step and one weight step above its own label — never one or the other.
The Tone Before Lift RuleSeparate surfaces by tone first. Shadows are a last resort, and they are #163628, never black.
The Radius Scales With Size RuleIcon badge 8 → control 14 → card 16 → hero 20 → panel 24. Never a 24pt chip or an 8pt panel.
The Lifted Panel RuleThe body container overlaps the header by 20pt with 24pt top corners. It never gains a shadow, a border, or bottom rounding.
The No Shouting RuleNo uppercase except the hero eyebrow. Bold is for titles and money only, never for mid-sentence emphasis.
This preview now matches the app
  • The header is a flat #2F6F57 fill. A gradient was implemented and reverted — react-native-linear-gradient mis-measures the header on this RN version and clips the header row under the status bar.
  • Press response. Buttons spring to scale(0.97) on press-in and release back to 1.
  • Contrast. Semantic colors are fills only; text uses the darkened variants (#B3261E, #15803D, #B45309, #1D4ED8), and bright fills carry Receipt Ink rather than white.
